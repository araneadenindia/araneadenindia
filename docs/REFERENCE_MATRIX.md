## Strategic Synthesis for ARANEA DEN

1. **From Pear:** Borrow the chapter-based narrative architecture, the high-contrast inscriptional editorial typography, and the deliberate use of fine art/mathematical metaphor.
2. **From Camille Mormal:** Borrow the quiet luxury, the absence of generic template cards, and the fluid, physics-based inertial pacing.
3. **From United Carriers:** Borrow the technical telemetry aesthetic, the mono-spaced metadata labels, and the authoritative dark-mode canvas.
4. **From Flowlife:** Borrow the floating glass pill navigation island and the high-contrast pairing of deep monochrome with a single emotive red accent.
5. **From Arc'teryx:** Borrow the hierarchical utility navigation structure and the obsessive technical classification runes/badges.
6. **From Komma Komma:** Borrow the confident studio manifesto rhythm, the horizontal project metadata rail, and the energetic warm off-black background relationship.
7. **From Stereoscope:** Borrow the sensory restraint, the multi-sensory immersion, and the serene spatial whitespace.
8. **From Bleibtgleich:** Borrow the rigid hairline architectural grid dividers, the ultra-tight typography tracking, and the uncompromising Swiss graphic foundation.
